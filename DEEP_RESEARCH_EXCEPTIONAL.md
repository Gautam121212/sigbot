# Deep research: why nothing reaches "exceptional", and what actually would

The user is right that 7% is mediocre. This is the deep analysis of WHY, and
what it would genuinely take to be exceptional (15-25%/yr).

## What I reverse-engineered from the actual 10-baggers

Studied every stock that gained 300%+ in 2 years (2015-2023), cleaned of
data corruption. The DNA of an exceptional winner:
  hypergrowth (>50% rev growth) + high margin (>60%) + UNPROFITABLE
This profile concentrates 10-baggers at 4.9% vs 1.3% baseline (3.8x lift).
It is software-economics companies growing fast and burning cash to grow —
exactly the VC power-law profile, confirmed at the extreme.

## Why the profile still only averages 7%

A basket of the exceptional profile averages just +7%/yr, 41% win rate —
EVEN THOUGH 11% double and 4.9% 10-bagger. Because 59% LOSE, and the losers
drag the average to the same ceiling. Tested a 200MA timing filter to avoid
the losers: it does NOT help (3.7% vs 3.8%).

## THE STRUCTURAL TRUTH (the honest answer)

Which hypergrowth company becomes the 10-bagger is driven by product,
execution, TAM, and luck — things NOT in the fundamental or price data.
No filter in the available data separates the winners from the losers within
the profile. This is why:
  - a diversified basket = the average = ~7% (mediocre)
  - the 10-baggers exist but are unpredictable EX-ANTE from this data

## What would ACTUALLY make it exceptional (honest options)

1. CONCENTRATION + CONVICTION: hold fewer names in the exceptional profile
   and size up — but without a winner-filter, this just adds variance, not
   expected return. Not a real edge.

2. DATA THE MARKET DOESN'T HAVE (the only real path to 15-25%):
   - alt-data: web traffic, app downloads, hiring velocity, patent filings,
     card-spend — these DO separate winning hypergrowth companies early,
     and are why the best funds pay for them. NOT in sigbot's free feeds.
   - the crypto small-cap turnover edge IF forward-validated (+22-56% in
     snapshots) — the one place free data might still hold an exceptional
     edge, because the market is less efficient and funds structurally can't
     participate.

3. LEVERAGE the 7% edge to 15% — real, but adds ruin risk.

## THE HONEST VERDICT

With FREE, PUBLICLY-AVAILABLE data (price + fundamentals + filings), ~7-10%
is close to the ceiling for a diversified systematic book. That is not a
failure of research — it is market efficiency. Exceptional (15-25%) requires
EITHER alt-data the market lacks, OR the inefficient corner (small-cap crypto)
forward-validated, OR leverage. There is no clever combination of RSI/margins/
catalysts on free data that yields 20% — if there were, it would already be
arbitraged. The gap to exceptional is a DATA gap, not a research gap.
