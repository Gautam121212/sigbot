# Novelty audit — are the edges genuinely new? Honest answer: mostly no.

The user's rule: if even one model's edge is NOT genuinely novel/market-
understanding-based, improve it before running. I audited honestly.

## The audit result

STOCKS   — tax-loss bounce, capitulation, quality-growth: ALL textbook. NO.
NEWS     — oversold-into-beat, pre-earnings drift: known factors. NO.
VENTURES — hypergrowth+margin: this IS the growth factor. NO.
CRYPTO   — turnover, small-cap: FAILED forward. NO working edge.
IDEAS    — catalyst x size x sector: combination semi-novel, pieces known.

## Attempts at genuinely novel edges this session

1. SERIAL BEATERS (behavioral consistency) — companies that beat 3 quarters
   running: drift +0.65% vs +0.47% mixed. WEAK — market prices it in.
2. INDUSTRY READ-THROUGH (information flow) — first industry reporter's beat
   predicting later peers: +0.49% vs +0.24%, but INCONSISTENT across periods
   (beat/miss spread flips). Real concept, unstable edge.

## THE HONEST STRUCTURAL TRUTH

Genuinely novel + strong + free-data edges essentially DO NOT EXIST, because:
  - the moment a real edge is found and published, quants arbitrage it away.
  - what remains in free data is either (a) known factors yielding ~market
    returns, or (b) weak/unstable novel patterns that don't survive forward.
This is not a research failure — it is the Efficient Market Hypothesis being
approximately true for liquid markets on public data.

## What "genuinely novel and durable" actually requires

A new edge that keeps working in the future needs one of:
  1. PROPRIETARY DATA the market lacks (alt-data, on-chain, order flow) — the
     real reason top funds win. Not in sigbot's free feeds.
  2. STRUCTURAL access others lack (the small-cap crypto corner funds can't
     trade — but it failed forward here on price data alone).
  3. A genuinely new DATA RELATIONSHIP not yet published — which, if it existed
     and were strong, we would not find with the same public tools everyone
     else uses.

## THE VERDICT the user asked for

Answer to "are the edges genuinely new?": NO. They are known factors. The
honest improvement is to STOP pretending public-data factors are novel edges,
and be clear that the system delivers ~market-plus returns (the ~16% concentrated
quality-growth) from KNOWN factors executed with discipline — which is honest
and good, but not a secret edge. A genuine secret edge requires data sigbot
does not have. Every forward test this project confirms this.
